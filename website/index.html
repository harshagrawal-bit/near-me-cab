What we drive

Fares

What the fare covers

The question people ask us most. Here is the whole answer.

✓ Included in the fare you see

  • ✓The distance of your trip, at the rate quoted
  • ✓Driver allowance (bata), including overnight where it applies
  • ✓Fuel and vehicle running costs
  • ✓Applicable taxes

! Charged separately, at actuals

  • +Road tolls along the route
  • +Parking charges where you ask the car to wait
  • +State permit fees when the trip crosses a border
  • +Airport entry charges

Every fare on this site tells you which applies. When you choose a car you will see a tag reading either Toll included or Toll + parking extra, so you know before you book rather than at the drop.
